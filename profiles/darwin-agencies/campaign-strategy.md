## Core Positioning — Why Agencies Are a Different Pitch

Agencies understand sales, marketing, and client acquisition conceptually — they do it for clients. The pitch to an agency is not "let us teach you marketing." It is:

> "You're brilliant at getting results for your clients but you're running your own new business on referrals and hustle. DGK builds the system so your agency wins clients the way your best clients win theirs."

This is the **cobbler's children** angle — the agency that has no shoes. It works because:
1. It's observationally true and most agency owners admit it privately
2. It positions DGK as a peer, not a vendor
3. It reframes the offer as the thing the agency owner knows they should have but hasn't built

## Front-End Offer Suggestions

### 1. Lead Magnet: "Agency New Business Blueprint"
Offers the new client acquisition framework before asking for a call. Peer-level value, low-friction CTA.

### 2. Agency Competitor Analysis
DGK analyses 3 Darwin competitors in their category — how they're positioned on Google, their review strategy, their website's conversion elements. Delivered as a short Gamma report. Specific, immediately useful.

### 3. New Business Audit Call (30 min)
DGK reviews how the agency currently wins clients, maps the referral dependency, and gives 3 specific actions to build a pipeline. No pitch on the call.
