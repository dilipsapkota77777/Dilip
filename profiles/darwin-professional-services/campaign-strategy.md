## Core Pain — Why This Segment Buys

Professional services firms in Darwin share one universal pain point: **the referral ceiling**.

- Revenue is stable but growth is invisible — they can't predict next month's revenue
- Every new client came from someone they already know
- When referrals slow down (Q3/Q4, post-holiday, economic uncertainty), pipeline dries up
- They know they should be doing "marketing" but don't have the time, budget, or system

**The insight DGK leads with:** The referral ceiling is not a marketing problem — it's a pipeline system problem. The firms growing past it aren't advertising more; they've built a client acquisition system that runs without them.

## Front-End Offer Suggestions

### 1. Lead Magnet: "The 5-Step Client Pipeline"
Offers the client acquisition framework before asking for a call. Low-friction ask.

### 2. Free Google Presence Audit
DGK audits their Google Business Profile — rating, review velocity, how they rank vs Darwin competitors in their category. Delivers a personalised report. Concrete and immediately valuable.

### 3. Pipeline Audit Call (30 min)
DGK diagnoses their current referral system live — how much comes from word-of-mouth, what a 3-month drought looks like, 3 specific actions to fix it. No pitch required on the call.
