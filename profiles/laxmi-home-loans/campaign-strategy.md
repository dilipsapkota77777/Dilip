## Customer Discovery Analysis

**Note:** Website (mortgagepros.com.au) returned 403 — analysis based on ICP onboarding interview and Australian mortgage broker industry patterns.

**Typical best-fit referral partners for a boutique mortgage broker:**

| Partner Type | Industry | Size | Key Metric | Problem They Have |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Independent real estate agency | Real Estate | 2–10 staff | 20–80 listings/year | Buyers fall through because finance not sorted early |
| Boutique buyer's agency | Real Estate | 1–5 staff | 30–100 transactions/year | Clients ask "can you recommend a broker?" and they have no one reliable |
| Small accounting firm (SMSF focus) | Accounting | 2–15 staff | 50–200 clients | SMSF clients want to buy property, accountant has no trusted broker referral |
| Financial planning practice | Financial Services | 2–20 staff | 100–300 clients | Clients asking about home loans outside their licence scope |
| Nepali-owned agency | Real Estate / Accounting | 1–15 staff | Multicultural client base | Need a broker who speaks the same language as their clients |

**Patterns:**
- Best partners are independent (not franchise HQ) — they have discretion to choose their own referral partners
- Buyer's agents have the highest referral volume and most direct need for fast pre-quals
- Accountants with SMSF clients have the highest ACV per referral (investment properties = larger loans = higher trail)
- Nepali-owned professional services firms are an underserved, high-trust niche for Laxmi specifically

**Targeting Recommendation:**
Base ICP (real estate + accounting, 1–50) is correct. Expand to include **buyer's agents** as a distinct high-priority sub-segment — they refer more frequently than traditional sales agents and their entire business model depends on buyers getting finance. Also consider **migration agents** as a secondary niche — they work directly with new arrivals (including Nepali community) who need mortgages.

**Best Lookalike Anchor Profiles:**
1. Independent buyer's agency, 1–5 staff, Sydney/Melbourne/Brisbane
2. Boutique accounting firm with SMSF services, 3–15 staff, suburban location
3. Nepali-owned real estate or accounting firm, any major Australian city

## Front-End Offer Suggestions

### 1. The 24-Hour Pre-Qual (Already chosen — highest priority for agents)
Send prospects a free, no-obligation pre-qualification for one of their current buyers. They experience the service before committing to a referral relationship. **This is your best front-end offer.**

### 2. The SMSF Lending Explainer PDF
A 1–2 page PDF covering:
- What SMSF members can and can't borrow for
- 3 common LRBA mistakes that get loans declined
- When to refer to a specialist broker vs. handle in-house
Write once, send forever. Accountants will save it and share it internally.

### 3. Broker Comparison Checklist — "10 Questions to Ask Your Mortgage Broker"
A checklist referral partners can use to vet any broker (designed so Laxmi passes all 10). This is subtle competitive positioning — every question is one Laxmi can answer confidently.
Example questions:
- "How many lenders are on your panel?"
- "What's your average time from application to formal approval?"
- "Do you handle SMSF/LRBA loans?"
- "What's your first-submission approval rate?"

### 4. Suburb Buyer Finance Report (High personalisation, high effort)
A one-paragraph personalised note showing average pre-approval times and buyer sentiment in the suburbs an agent actively lists in. High effort to produce but extremely relevant — pair with Campaign #3 (Settlement Speed Report) for high-ACV targets.
