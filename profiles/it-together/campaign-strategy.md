# Campaign Strategy — IT Together
Generated: 2026-06-21

**Target Audience:** Business owners, ops managers, IT managers, and educators at 5–100 person Australian firms (all industries + education), no internal IT team
**Core Value Proposition:** Enterprise-grade managed IT, cybersecurity, and cloud support without the cost of a full-time hire — Australian-based team, fast response
**Key Differentiators:** Australian-based support, SMB-focused, Microsoft 365 expertise, fast onboarding, no lock-in contracts

## Customer Discovery Analysis

**Note:** Website blocked (403) — analysis based on ICP interview + Australian MSP industry patterns.

**Typical best-fit clients for a Sydney-based SMB-focused MSP:**

| Client Type | Industry | Size | Key Problem | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accounting firm | Accounting | 10–30 staff | Constant spam + no one to call when things break | Managed support + email security |
| Legal practice | Legal | 5–25 staff | Data breach risk + compliance pressure | Cybersecurity + managed backup |
| Allied health clinic | Healthcare | 10–50 staff | Patient data exposure + unreliable systems | Managed IT + cybersecurity |
| Construction company | Construction | 20–80 staff | Staff in field + office, no IT coordination | Cloud + device management |
| School / college | Education | 30–200 staff | Aging infrastructure + limited budget + tender process | Managed IT + infrastructure |
| Fast-growing SMB | Any | 15–60 staff | Hired 10 people in 6 months, IT can't keep up | Full managed IT takeover |

**Patterns:**
- Best clients have 10–50 staff, run Microsoft 365, and have no dedicated IT person
- Education clients have longer sales cycles but higher contract values and loyalty
- The SEEK signal (trying to hire IT staff) is the single most predictive trigger — it means pain is live and they've already decided they need help
- Healthcare and legal have compliance pressure that accelerates decisions

**Targeting Recommendation:**
All-industry approach is correct. Layer triggers on top (especially SEEK + no IT provider + M365 detected) rather than filtering by industry. The best-fit client is defined by their IT situation, not their industry.

**Best Lookalike Anchors:** 10–40 person accounting/legal/healthcare firms in Sydney running M365, with no IT company listed on their website.

## Front-End Offer Suggestions

### 1. Free Cybersecurity Dark Web Scan (Already chosen — highest priority)
Automated, immediate results, prospects are always surprised. Best opener across all campaigns targeting unmanaged businesses.

### 2. Free IT Health Check (Already chosen — strong fallback)
Works for any audience. Positions IT Together as already doing the work before they've paid.

### 3. "IT Readiness Score" (New idea — high potential)
A simple 10-question survey (Google Form) that scores their IT readiness 1–100 and auto-generates a one-page report. Shareable, reusable, and self-qualifying — only people with IT pain will fill it out.
- **Delivery:** Google Form + automated Google Doc output. Build once.
- **CTA:** "Take our 2-minute IT readiness quiz — get your score instantly."
- **Why it works:** Self-service lead qualification. High scorers (well-managed) self-exclude. Low scorers (IT pain) are hot leads.

### 4. "What Does IT Really Cost Your Business?" Landing Page (Supporting asset)
A simple calculator page: team size × average hourly rate × downtime hours/month = cost of current IT approach. Include a comparison bar: "This vs. IT Together per month."
- Pair with the Downtime Calculator magnet for email campaigns.
- Can be linked from any email as a soft CTA.
