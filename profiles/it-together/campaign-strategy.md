## Segment A — CFO / Finance Director

**Pain:** Overpaying for IT headcount OR underserved by break-fix and not realising the true cost
**Buying trigger:** Currently has full-time IT hire, OR just had an unexpected IT cost
**Proof point to use:** Cost comparison — managed IT vs full-time IT hire at their headcount

### Email 1 — Do the Math (CFO)

```
Subject: it hire

{{firstName}},

Quick back-of-napkin at {{company}}'s headcount:

Full-time IT hire = ~$85–$95k salary + super + tools + cover days + recruitment.

Managed IT with proactive monitoring = fraction of that — and covers 24/7 with no gaps.

Most CFOs we work with save 30–45% in year one.

Worth running the actual numbers for your setup?
```

*Framework: Do the Math | Value prop: Show Cost | CTA: Soft reply ask*

### Email 2 — Specific Outcome (CFO)

```

One more thing I left out — cost isn't just headcount.

Proactive monitoring catches most issues before users notice.

We operate on a 99.9% uptime SLA with a 15-minute response guarantee.
```

*Framework: Specific Outcome | Value prop: Uptime + SLA | CTA: 15-min call*

## Anti-Spam Checklist

- [ ] 2-word subject lines, no spam trigger words
- [ ] Plain text only (no images, HTML, logos)
- [ ] Unsubscribe link in footer (Spam Act 2003 compliance)
- [ ] Reply-based CTA only — no booking links in Email 1
- [ ] Sending from warmed domain (not ittogether.com.au primary)
- [ ] Max 30-40 emails/inbox/day
- [ ] No "free", "limited time", "act now", "click here"
