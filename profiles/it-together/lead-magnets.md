## Top Picks by Campaign

### #1 — Cybersecurity / Dark Web Risk Scan (17/20)
**What it is:** Automated scan of their business domain showing exposed credentials, email security gaps (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), and dark web exposure.
**Delivery:** Tools like HaveIBeenPwned API, MXToolbox, SecurityScorecard — results in <24 hours.
**Example CTA:**
> "Reply with your business domain and I'll run a free cybersecurity scan — takes 2 minutes, results back within 24 hours."
**Best for:** Cybersecurity campaigns, companies with no IT provider on website

---

### #2 — Hiring vs MSP Cost Calculator (16/20)
**What it is:** Personalised estimate: cost of 1 full-time IT Support hire in Sydney (salary ~$65–80k + super + recruitment + productivity loss during vacancy) vs IT Together monthly fee.
**Delivery:** Google Sheet or simple web calculator — built once, personalised per reply.
**Example CTA:**
> "Reply and I'll send you the actual numbers — most businesses save 40–60% vs hiring in-house."
**Best for:** SEEK hiring signal campaigns exclusively (companies actively trying to hire IT staff)

---

### #3 — Free IT Health Check (16/20)
**What it is:** Review of their current IT setup — email security, patch status, backup coverage, MFA adoption — returned as a one-page report.
**Delivery:** Semi-automated using standard toolset. 20–30 min per check.
**Example CTA:**
> "Reply and I'll send you a free IT health check report for your business. No commitment, no sales call required."
**Best for:** Broad catch-all campaigns, fallback for any sequence

---

### #4 — IT Downtime Cost Calculator (15/20)
**What it is:** Simple calculator personalised with their team size and average hourly rate, showing what 1 hour of IT downtime costs — most SMBs are shocked by the number.
**Delivery:** Google Sheet, personalised per reply (2 min).
**Example CTA:**
> "Reply with your team size and I'll send back a personalised downtime cost estimate — most SMBs are shocked by the number."
**Best for:** Operations Manager / Business Owner campaigns
