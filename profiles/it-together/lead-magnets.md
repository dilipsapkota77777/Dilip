### IT Risk Snapshot — 5 Questions for Australian Business Owners

*Takes 2 minutes. Identifies your top 3 IT exposure points.*

---

**Question 1: How quickly can you get IT support when something breaks?**
- [ ] A — Within minutes (dedicated support contract)
- [ ] B — Same day (IT person on staff)
- [ ] C — Next day or longer (break-fix / ad-hoc)
- [ ] D — We figure it out ourselves

**Question 2: When did you last review your cybersecurity setup?**
- [ ] A — Within the last 12 months
- [ ] B — 1–2 years ago
- [ ] C — More than 2 years ago
- [ ] D — Never / not sure

**Question 3: Do you have a tested backup and recovery plan?**
- [ ] A — Yes, tested in the last 6 months
- [ ] B — Yes, but haven't tested it
- [ ] C — We back up data but no formal recovery plan
- [ ] D — No backup plan

**Question 4: Do your staff receive any cybersecurity training?**
- [ ] A — Quarterly or more
- [ ] B — Annually
- [ ] C — Once (onboarding only)
- [ ] D — Never

**Question 5: Have you experienced an IT outage or security incident in the last 12 months?**
- [ ] A — No incidents
- [ ] B — Minor incident, resolved quickly
- [ ] C — Significant incident, cost us time/money
- [ ] D — Major incident (data loss, extended downtime, breach)

---

### Scoring Guide (include this in the PDF)

**Mostly A:** Low risk. Your IT setup is solid — focus on staying proactive as you scale.

**Mostly B:** Moderate risk. A few gaps that don't hurt yet but will as you grow. Worth a conversation.

**Mostly C:** Elevated risk. You're running on assumptions. One incident could cost you significantly.

**Mostly D:** High risk. If something goes wrong today, recovery will be slow and expensive. Act now.

---

### What to Ask Your Current IT Provider

Use these questions in your next vendor review or renewal:

1. What is your contractual response time for P1 incidents?
2. Do you monitor our environment 24/7 or business hours only?
3. Is cybersecurity bundled or a separate cost?
4. Can you show me our uptime report for the last 12 months?
5. What percentage of our issues last quarter were caught proactively vs reactively?

> *"If your provider can't answer Question 5 — that's your answer."*
