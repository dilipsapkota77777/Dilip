# Lead Magnets — IT Together

## The Problem
IT Together has no published lead magnet or free entry offer. Cold email CTAs like "contact us to discuss" are too vague and low-converting. Every sequence needs a low-friction, specific reason to reply.

---

## Chosen Primary Lead Magnet

### Free Network & Security Assessment (45 minutes)
**CTA phrasing:** "Worth a free 45-min network and security assessment?"
**What happens:** IT Together runs a structured review of the prospect's current IT environment — network topology, security posture, backup status, and any obvious vulnerabilities. At the end, they deliver a 1-page summary of findings with 3 prioritised actions.
**Why it works:**
- Specific and credible (not a "free consultation" — an actual assessment)
- Immediately surfaces the pain (most SMBs have at least one glaring gap)
- Positions IT Together as expert before any commercial conversation
- The gap findings ARE the sales pitch — no pitch needed
**Delivery:** Remote (screen share + questions), 45 min, Calendly link
**Cost to IT Together:** ~1 hour of a senior engineer's time per prospect

---

## Backup Options

### Option 2: Free Cybersecurity Risk Report
**CTA:** "I can send you a free cybersecurity risk report for [company name] — takes 5 minutes on our end."
**What it is:** Automated external scan of their domain (MX records, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, SSL cert, dark web email exposure via HaveIBeenPwned). Delivered as a branded PDF.
**Why:** Zero calendar friction. Scales without engineer time. Shows a gap instantly.
**Best for:** High-volume sequences where booking calls at scale isn't viable.

### Option 3: "AI-Ready IT Scorecard"
**CTA:** "Curious how AI-ready your IT infrastructure actually is? I'll send you a 5-question scorecard."
**What it is:** Short Typeform that scores their IT across 5 dimensions relevant to AI adoption (security, cloud readiness, data hygiene, automation maturity, support coverage). Automated score + PDF with gaps.
**Why:** Directly leverages IT Together's AI differentiator. Unique angle vs every other MSP.
**Best for:** Campaigns targeting companies actively trying to adopt AI tools (Copilot, ChatGPT, automation workflows).

### Option 4: Free IT Vendor Audit
**CTA:** "Most businesses are paying for 3–5 IT vendors that overlap. Want a free audit of what you can consolidate?"
**What it is:** 30-min call reviewing their current IT vendor stack. IT Together maps overlaps and estimates the savings from consolidation.
**Why:** Cost-saving framing. Bypasses "we already have IT sorted" objection.
**Best for:** Businesses that already have an MSP but are over-paying or frustrated.

---

## Recommendation
Launch with **Free Network & Security Assessment** for all campaigns. Switch to **Free Cybersecurity Risk Report** (automated) once volume gets high enough that booking 45-min calls creates a bottleneck.

Use **AI-Ready IT Scorecard** specifically for the AI-readiness campaign angle (Campaign #8 in strategy doc).
