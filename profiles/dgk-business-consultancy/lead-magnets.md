# Lead Magnets — DGK Business Consultancy

## Chosen Primary Lead Magnet

### Pipeline Audit Call (30 minutes, free)
**CTA phrasing:** "Worth a free 30-min pipeline audit?"
**What happens:** Dilip gets on a call, asks 5-6 questions about their current lead sources, conversion rates, and where deals are stalling. At the end, gives them 3 specific actions they can take immediately — whether they work with DGK or not.
**Why it works:** Low commitment, immediate value, positions DGK as expert before any pitch. Surfaces the infrastructure gap naturally without selling it.
**Delivery:** Calendly link, 30 min Zoom

---

## Backup Options (if pipeline audit call underperforms)

### Option 2: Outbound Readiness Scorecard
**CTA:** "I'll send you a 5-question scorecard that shows exactly where your pipeline is leaking."
**What it is:** A short Typeform/Google Form that scores their pipeline across 5 dimensions. Automated PDF report at the end. Low-friction, scales without Dilip's time.
**Best for:** High-volume sequences where you can't do 1:1 calls at scale.

### Option 3: "No Referral" PDF Playbook
**CTA:** "Want the exact framework we used to take an IT firm from 0 to 6 enquiries/month in 60 days? I'll send it over."
**What it is:** 6-8 page PDF. Real playbook — ICP definition, list building, sequence structure, follow-up cadence. Shows the methodology without giving away the done-with-you execution.
**Best for:** Skeptical founders who want proof before a call.

### Option 4: Hiring Signal Sequence (show don't tell)
**CTA:** "I can show you the exact 3-email sequence we used to trigger same-week responses from financial planning firms using SEEK signals."
**What it is:** The literal sequence (lightly anonymised). Works as social proof AND as a tangible deliverable.
**Best for:** Outbound/lead gen focused campaigns targeting founders who already know they need outbound.

### Option 5: Free CRM Audit
**CTA:** "30 minutes. I'll look at your CRM and tell you what's breaking your follow-up."
**What it is:** Screen-share review of their HubSpot/Salesforce/spreadsheet. Specific, actionable, shows the gap DGK fills.
**Best for:** Accounting, legal, financial planning — service firms who have a CRM but don't use it properly.

---

## Recommendation

Start with **Pipeline Audit Call** across all campaigns. Switch to **Outbound Readiness Scorecard** if call-booking volume is too low to handle or if you want to run higher-volume sequences without calendar dependency.
