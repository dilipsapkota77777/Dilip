## No-AI Campaign

**Campaign: NT Word of Mouth — Static**

Why it works without AI: Every NT trade business relies on word of mouth. This truth is universal — no personalisation needed when the pain is this universal.

Core message structure:
- Line 1: "Most Darwin trade businesses get new clients through who they know — not what they show online."
- Line 2: "Put together a short guide on what the ones with consistent commercial enquiries are doing differently."
- CTA: "Can I send it over?"
- PS: [Google reviews data if available — Clayscript, 0 credits]

This works because the hook is the same for every trade business in Darwin. The pain is identical. No AI needed.

## Front-End Offer Suggestions

### 1. Lead Magnet: "Get More Commercial Contracts Without Ads"
**Rationale:** Offers real value (the 5-step system) before asking for a call. Low-friction ask in Email 1 ("Can I send it over?") converts to a conversation, not a meeting — lower resistance.

### 2. Free Google Business Audit
**Rationale:** DGK can audit their Google Business Profile (rating, review velocity, photos, posts, keywords) and send a personalised report showing exactly where they rank vs competitors in Darwin. Specific, visual, immediately valuable — makes the offer concrete before the call.
**How to deliver:** Claygent can check their Google Business Profile status and generate a basic audit score automatically from their Google Maps link (already in CSV).

### 3. Darwin Digital Benchmark Report
**Rationale:** "See how your Google presence compares to other NT trade businesses at your revenue level." Benchmarking works because every business owner wants to know where they stand vs their market.
**How to deliver:** Build a simple Gamma doc with industry averages from the CSV data (average rating, review count, digital presence score by industry group).
