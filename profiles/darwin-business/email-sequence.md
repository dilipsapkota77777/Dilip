## Personalisation System

### The Google Reviews Signal

From the CSV, each contact has:
- `Rating` (e.g. 4.8, 5.0, 3.9)
- `Reviews` (review count)
- `Google Reviews Opener` (pre-generated text)

**Signal logic:**
- Rating ≥ 4.5 AND Reviews ≥ 20 → "Strong trust signal, low digital conversion"
- Rating ≥ 4.0 AND Reviews ≥ 5 → "Solid reputation, room to amplify"
- Rating < 4.0 OR Reviews < 5 → "Reviews need work first before amplifying"

**Pre-generated `Google Reviews Opener` examples from CSV:**
- Boka Electrical (5.0, 15 reviews): "5.0 from 15 reviews, you've built trust—are your online enquiries converting it?"
- VTG Waste (4.0, 13 reviews): "4 from 13 reviews suggests good work, but the digital signal doesn't show it yet."
- Sitzler (4.0, 5 reviews): "4 from 5 reviews suggests good work, but the digital signal doesn't show it yet."

### The Hiring Signal

140 of 256 contacts are actively hiring. Hiring = growing = capacity coming before clients.
This is used in `signal_tier` scoring (hiring = Warm or Hot).
